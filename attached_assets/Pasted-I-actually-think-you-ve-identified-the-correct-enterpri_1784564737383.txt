## Database fields to support this

For each vendor-product combination, store:

```text
current_stock
reserved_stock
available_stock (computed = current_stock - reserved_stock)
incoming_stock

allocated_qty
po_pending_qty
po_created_qty

last_stock_update

auto_allocate_enabled
preferred_vendor

unit_price
moq
lead_time_days

vendor_score

This design gives you:

Accurate stock visibility for all clients.
No overselling, because reserved stock immediately reduces shop availability.
Vendor inventory is only physically consumed when a PO is raised.
Automatic restoration of stock on cancellation before PO.
Intelligent, weighted auto-allocation that still allows procurement users to override quantities manually.

This is the same architectural pattern used by large procurement and order orchestration platforms because it separates customer reservation from supplier commitment, making inventory tracking far more reliable.
