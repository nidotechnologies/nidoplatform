PART 2 – PROCUREMENT, PURCHASE ORDERS, AND SYSTEM CHANGES
Phase 5 – Automatically Create Draft Purchase Orders

Immediately after the order is successfully created:

Automatically generate Draft Purchase Orders grouped by Vendor.

Example:

Order ORD-000012

Laptop -> Vendor A
Mouse -> Vendor B

Generate:

PO-00051 (Draft) Vendor A
PO-00052 (Draft) Vendor B

Each Draft PO contains:

Vendor
Products
Quantities
Prices
Taxes
Delivery Address
Client Details

Status:

Draft

Do NOT send to vendor.
Do NOT email vendor.
Do NOT notify vendor.

Phase 6 – Procurement Review

Add a new Draft Purchase Orders workflow.

Admin opens Draft PO.

Admin can:

Review products
Review quantities
Review pricing
Review taxes
Review stock
Edit notes
Approve
Reject
Cancel

The selected vendor cannot be changed here unless the Draft PO is cancelled and recreated through a controlled reallocation process.

Phase 7 – Confirm Purchase Order

When Admin clicks "Confirm Purchase Order":

Status changes from Draft -> Confirmed
Generate Final PDF
Send Email to Vendor
Send Vendor Notification
Create Audit Log
Convert Reserved Stock -> Allocated Stock
Lock PO for further edits

Only after confirmation should procurement commitment become final.

Remove These Features

Remove Vendor Selection Engine execution after Place Order.

Remove Recommended Vendor Allocation from Order Details.

Remove Vendor Recommendation popup.

Remove Cheapest First Fill from Order workflow.

Remove Composite Vendor Scoring during order creation.

Vendor allocation should already be completed by the client during shopping.

Validation Rules
Vendor selection is mandatory before Add to Cart.
Add to Cart is disabled until a vendor is selected.
Reserved stock belongs only to the selected vendor.
Draft Purchase Orders are automatically generated after successful order placement.
Draft Purchase Orders must never be sent to vendors automatically.
Vendors receive Purchase Orders only after procurement confirmation.
All stock reservations must remain consistent from Shop to Delivery.
Preserve complete audit history for vendor selection, stock reservations, Draft PO creation, and PO confirmation.

Implement these changes across the UI, backend APIs, database models, business logic, and workflow while preserving all existing functionality that is not explicitly modified.
