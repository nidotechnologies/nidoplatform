5. DIGITAL VENDOR/SUPPLIER

Do not remove the vendor relationship from Digital Products.

Digital Products may still have an internal supplier/vendor.

The difference is:

Physical Product: customer selects the vendor.
Digital Product: NIDO internally uses the configured default/preferred supplier.

The customer must never be required to select the Digital supplier.

Reuse the existing Vendor/Product relationship.

Only add a preferred/default supplier field if the existing system does not already support this.

Do not create duplicate supplier structures.

For Digital Order Items, store the internal supplier/vendor snapshot if the existing architecture supports vendor snapshots.
